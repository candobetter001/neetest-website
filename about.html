About

A focused practice tool, built by someone who sat the exam.

No videos. No mentors. No leaderboards. Just an MCQ bank and a dashboard that tells you the truth about where you stand.

Why this exists

I built NEETest after my own PG prep convinced me that most of the commercial platforms are selling the wrong thing.

They sell hours. Video hours, live-class hours, doubt-solving hours. But the marks come from a different place entirely — sitting with a question bank, getting things wrong, finding out specifically which things you keep getting wrong, and doing it again the next day.

I noticed I’d watch eight hours of lectures and my mock scores wouldn’t move. I’d sit with two hundred MCQs and a notebook over a weekend and my scores would jump. The expensive thing was decorative. The cheap thing was doing the work.

So the question I asked was: what would the cheap thing look like if it was built well? Not bigger. Not flashier. Just very, very well-made for the one job it has.

What NEETest does

  • A growing bank of MCQs, tagged by subject, year, and exam. Practice in topic-mode or take a timed mock.
  • A dashboard that updates after every attempt — accuracy, time-per-question, subject heatmap, weakest topics.
  • Bookmarks, wrong-answer drill, exam countdowns for NEET PG and INI-CET.

What NEETest does not do

No video lectures. No live classes. No one-on-one mentoring. No textbook chapters. No doubt solving. No college counselling. No rank guarantees. No leaderboards. No streak gamification.

If you want any of those, you’re not the wrong person — this is the wrong tool. Look elsewhere, honestly.
