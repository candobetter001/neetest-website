Why we built this

I'm Abhishek, a doctor preparing for NEET PG. After spending months looking at the prep landscape, the verdict was clear: everything good costs ₹25,000–₹50,000 per year. Free options were either ad-ridden, broken, or didn't cover PYQs properly.

That's wrong. Previous year questions are public exam material. They belong to every aspirant. Gating them behind paywalls is gatekeeping medicine itself — especially for students from Tier-2/3 cities who can't afford ₹50K courses.

So I built NEETest with one rule: PYQs are free, forever. Always. The premium tier funds the platform and unlocks analytics, smart tools, and full-length mocks — not the PYQs themselves.

What we promise

  • Free PYQs forever. No paywall, no signup wall, no "first 5 questions" trick.
  • Original content only. Every note, every explanation written from scratch citing standard textbooks. Zero copy-paste from any commercial PYQ provider.
  • Honest analytics. No inflated "strike rate" claims. Real numbers, your numbers.
  • Offline-first. Works in hostels with bad WiFi, on trains, in waiting rooms.
  • No dark patterns. No fake urgency, no exit-intent popups guilt-tripping you. We hate them too.
