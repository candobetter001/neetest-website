What NEETest is

NEETest is a self-paced MCQ practice tool and progress dashboard for aspirants preparing for NEET PG, INI-CET, and similar postgraduate medical entrance examinations.

It gives you two things:

  • A growing bank of previous-year MCQs you can practice, tagged by subject, year, and exam.
  • A dashboard that shows your accuracy, time taken, and weak subject areas.

What NEETest is not

We do not offer video lectures, live classes, one-on-one mentoring, textbook chapters, doubt solving, college counselling, rank guarantees, or any service other than the two above. If you want any of those, NEETest is not the right tool for you.
