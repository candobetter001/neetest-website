FAQ

Honest answers, including the awkward ones.

How are your MCQs sourced? +
Two sources, both legal. (1) Officially released previous-year papers from NBEMS, AIIMS, and the INI-CET conducting bodies — these are public-domain examination material. (2) Gap-filling MCQs we author ourselves based on standard textbooks (Robbins, Harrison, Gray’s, etc.), clearly tagged so you know which is which. We do not republish copyrighted material from any commercial prep platform.
Will lifetime really mean lifetime? +
Yes. If we shut the product down for any reason — we lose interest, we get acquired, the founder goes back to clinical practice — we will give 30 days’ written notice via email and provide a one-click export of your data (attempts, bookmarks, notes). You won’t wake up to a dead login.
How does this compare to the bigger platforms? +

Where we lose: we don’t have video lectures. We don’t have live doubt-solving with senior residents. We don’t have a built community or a Telegram group. If those things are core to how you study, the bigger platforms will serve you better — and at a much higher price.

Where we win: the question bank is sharper because we obsess over it. The dashboard is built around the math on the methodology page, not gamification. The price is ₹1,999 once, not ₹15,000+ per year. The Android app works on a basic phone over 2G.

Why ₹1,999 when the bigger platforms charge ₹15,000+? +
We don’t pay video producers, lecture presenters, doubt-solving residents, marketing teams, sales calls, or affiliate commissions. We don’t run TV ads or sponsor coaching centres. The product is one engineer’s evenings and weekends. The cost structure is genuinely lower — we’re passing that through, not subsidising a land-grab.
What if a question or explanation is wrong? +
Email candobetter001@gmail.com with the question ID (visible at the bottom of every question) and the issue. We review every report personally. Confirmed corrections push out in the next bi-weekly update and are logged on the changelog with the date.
Can I get a refund? +
Yes, within 7 days of purchase if you’ve attempted fewer than 20 questions. Full terms on the Refund Policy page. Email us; refunds are processed within 5 business days.
