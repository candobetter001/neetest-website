FAQ

Honest answers, including the awkward ones.

How are your MCQs sourced? +
Two sources, both legal. (1) Officially released previous-year papers from NBEMS, AIIMS, and the INI-CET conducting bodies — these are public-domain examination material. (2) Gap-filling MCQs we author ourselves based on standard textbooks (Robbins, Harrison, Gray’s, etc.), clearly tagged so you know which is which. We do not republish copyrighted material from any commercial prep platform.
Is it really free? What’s the catch? +
Yes, genuinely free — the full question bank, every mock test, and the complete dashboard, with no payment, no card, and no trial cap. No ads either. It’s in active beta and we’d rather get it into aspirants’ hands and hear what’s wrong than charge for it right now.
How does this compare to the bigger platforms? +

Where we lose: we don’t have video lectures. We don’t have live doubt-solving with senior residents. We don’t have a built community or a Telegram group. If those things are core to how you study, the bigger platforms will serve you better.

Where we win: the question bank is sharper because we obsess over it. The dashboard is built around the math on the methodology page, not gamification. It’s free, where they charge ₹15,000+ a year. And the Android app works on a basic phone over 2G.

Will you start charging later? +
Maybe, eventually, for new premium additions — but everything available today stays free, and we’ll always keep a genuinely useful free tier. If anything ever changes we’ll announce it clearly on the changelog well in advance. No surprise paywalls dropping mid-prep.
What if a question or explanation is wrong? +
Email candobetter001@gmail.com with the question ID (visible at the bottom of every question) and the issue. We review every report personally. Confirmed corrections push out in the next bi-weekly update and are logged on the changelog with the date.
Do I even need an account? +
No — you can try a mock as a guest with zero signup. An account (Gmail + OTP) only exists so your attempts, bookmarks and dashboard sync across your phone and laptop. It’s free too.
